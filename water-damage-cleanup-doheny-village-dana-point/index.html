Doheny Village is a mixed-use area of Dana Point centered on Doheny Park Road, in the Capistrano Beach part of the city. The city describes it as an eclectic combination of commercial, light industrial and residential uses.

Water damage cleanup in Doheny Village covers extraction, drying and moisture checks for homes and for small commercial or workshop spaces.

What the City Documents About Doheny Village

The Dana Point City Council adopted the Doheny Village Zoning Code Update on July 20, 2021. The California Coastal Commission approved the related amendment on February 9, 2023. The city says the update aims to preserve and enhance the area's mix of commercial, light industrial and residential uses.

Local reporting on the planning process places Doheny Park Road as the central corridor. The Pacific Coast Highway bridge over San Juan Creek is also nearby. The sources reviewed do not give exact boundaries, so this page describes the area approximately.

A Mixed Area Means Mixed Situations

A house on one block may sit across the street from a workshop. A leak in a shared building can affect a neighbor with different equipment and stock. The crew asks what each space is used for, since that changes what must be protected or removed.

For a business space, the work stays practical: extraction, removal of wet materials, drying and records. This page does not describe specific industries or customers.

Safety Steps in the First Hour

  • Close the fixture or main water valve if you can reach it safely.
  • Keep people and pets away from standing water, especially near outlets, cords or equipment.
  • Do not switch breakers while standing in water, and do not handle unknown chemicals that got wet.
  • Photograph the damage when it is safe, then call the line and say whether the space is a home or a business.

Extraction and Drying

1

Extract

Pumps and wet vacuums remove standing water from floors and carpet.

2

Find the wet areas

Moisture meters and a thermal camera trace water into walls, ceilings and slabs.

3

Remove what cannot dry

Soaked insulation, carpet pad and soft board are removed. Dry material stays.

4

Dry and record

Air movers and dehumidifiers run until readings are dry, and readings are logged.

Insurance Documentation

In a leased space, the lease and policies decide who covers what. In a home, read your own policy and ask your insurer. Photos, an inventory of damaged items and moisture readings can help describe the loss. They do not guarantee a claim will be approved.

Mistakes to Avoid

  • Reopening a business space on damp carpet or under wet ceiling tiles.
  • Throwing out stock or records before photographing them.
  • Using household fans or vacuums on sewage or runoff.
