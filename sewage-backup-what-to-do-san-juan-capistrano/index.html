Sewage Backup: What to Do Right Away in San Juan Capistrano, Near Dana Point

Sewage is contaminated water and the cleanup needs protective gear, containment and careful disposal. Here is what to do first.

By Dana Point Water Damage Pros · Updated October 29, 2026

Emergency Dispatch

Water coming up through a floor drain, a toilet or a tub, or a house that suddenly smells like sewage, is an emergency. The water may carry bacteria and other contaminants, so the first job is to keep people and pets away from it. This guide covers what to do in San Juan Capistrano and the surrounding area.

Our 24/7 line takes these calls, and same-day response is offered when crews are available. Cleanup is not a do-it-yourself job, and the plumbing repair is a separate trade.

Step One: Stay Out and Stop Adding Water

Do not flush toilets, run sinks, use the washing machine or run the dishwasher. Every drop you send down the drain may end up on your floor. Keep children and pets out of the room, and do not walk through the water, since you can carry contamination to other rooms on your shoes.

If the backup is from the main sewer line, other drains in the house may be affected, and neighbors might be too. If you can, check whether a neighbor has the same problem, because that points to the public sewer.

Step Two: Electrical Safety

Do not touch outlets, appliances or panels in or near the water. If the panel is dry and you can reach it without stepping in water, you may turn off breakers for the affected rooms. Otherwise call the electric utility or 911. Never run a gas appliance in a space affected by sewage or flooding.

Step Three: Call the Right People

  • The restoration line, for extraction, cleaning and drying.
  • A plumber, to clear the line or repair a broken pipe.
  • Your sewer or utility agency, if the problem looks like it is in the street main.
  • Your insurer, to start a claim and ask about sewer backup coverage.

What Federal Guidance Says About Sewage

The US Environmental Protection Agency's guidance on moisture and mold says water from sewage, or chemical or biological contamination, requires protective equipment and containment. It also says fans should not be used until the water is confirmed clean.

San Juan Capistrano is an old city, founded around the mission on November 1, 1776, and incorporated on April 19, 1961, with a Los Rios Street Historic District dating back well over a century. Older buildings and older lines mean more places for a backup to start, and a plumber can scope a line to find a root intrusion, a collapsed section or a blockage.

How Sewage Cleanup Works

The crew wears protective clothing, sets containment so contamination does not spread and extracts the water. Porous materials that sewage touched, such as carpet, pad, insulation and drywall, are usually removed and disposed of as contaminated waste. Hard surfaces are cleaned and disinfected, and the structure is dried with air movers and dehumidifiers.

The standard that guides this is ANSI/IICRC S500, which treats sewage as Category 3 water. After drying and cleaning, water damage repair rebuilds what was removed. The service most closely related is flood damage restoration.

Why Backups Happen

Common causes include roots entering a lateral line, grease and wipes building up in a drain, a collapsed or offset pipe and a blockage in the municipal main. Heavy use during a storm can add load to the system. A plumber can scope the line with a camera to see where the problem sits and whether it is on your side of the property line or the agency's side.

What Not to Do

Do not use household bleach on a large sewage spill and assume it is finished. Do not use a household wet vacuum on sewage, because it can spray contamination. Do not leave wet porous materials in place to save money, because they cannot be made safe. Do not use fans on the water until the crew says the area is contained.

Insurance Notes

Coverage for sewer backup is often an optional endorsement and not part of a standard homeowners policy. Ask your insurer what applies. Keep photos, the plumber's report and receipts, and keep a log of calls. Neighboring homeowners in Laguna Niguel and San Clemente can find the same checklist helpful.

Tell the dispatcher if anyone in the home is elderly, pregnant or has a weakened immune system, and ask a physician about health concerns, since this page does not answer medical questions.

Local Questions

Who fixes the sewer line?

A plumber clears or repairs the line. If the problem is in the street main, your sewer agency handles it. The restoration crew handles the water that came into the house.

Can I clean a small sewage spill myself?

Even small spills carry contamination. If you choose to, use protective gear and dispose of materials carefully. For anything beyond a very small area, call a crew.

Will my insurance cover sewer backup?

Often only with an optional endorsement. Ask your insurer what your policy says before a loss.

Are old homes in San Juan Capistrano more prone to backups?

Age is one factor, since older lines can have roots, breaks or blockages. A plumber can scope the line to find the cause.
