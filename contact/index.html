What to Have Ready When You Call

  • The address and the nearest cross street.
  • Where the water is and where it came from.
  • Whether you have shut off the water and power.
  • Whether any water looks dirty, smells like sewage or came from outside.
  • Your insurer's name, if you have started a claim.

Before You Call: Safety First

Stop the source if you can do so safely. Stay out of standing water near outlets or panels. Keep children and pets out of the affected area. If the ceiling is bulging, stay out of the room. If you smell gas or see sparks, leave and call 911. Dispatch will talk you through the rest.
