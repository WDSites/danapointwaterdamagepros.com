Tract Homes From the 1970s and 1980s

Mission Viejo incorporated in 1988 and is a master-planned community. Donald Bren's plan put roads in the valleys and houses on the hills, and by 1980 much of the city was complete. Tracts often sold out before construction began in the late 1970s and 1980s.

That means many homes are now between forty and fifty years old. Original copper or galvanized lines, water heaters, toilet supply hoses and shutoff valves have reached the end of their service life, and two-story houses carry water from upstairs to the rooms below. Tile roofs and stucco in the Spanish mission style add their own leak points.

When Water Is Spreading in Mission Viejo

Before We Arrive: If Water Is Still Spreading

1

Stop the source

Find the shutoff at the fixture, the water heater or the main, and close it.

2

Power

Turn off breakers to wet rooms only from a dry panel, and never touch outlets in standing water.

3

Lift and move

Lift furniture and electronics, roll up rugs and photograph the damage.

4

People and pets

Keep children and pets out of the water, and avoid rooms where the ceiling is bulging.

5

Call the line

Lakes, Creeks and Neighborhood Features

Lake Mission Viejo is an artificial lake of about a mile along Marguerite Parkway, open to a private residents' association. Oso Creek runs through the city, and the Oso Creek Trail follows it. Most homes are well away from water, but lots near the creek and the lake can see runoff in heavy rain.

Saddleback College sits in the southern half of the city and Mission Hospital is the largest hospital in south Orange County. Condos and townhomes near those corridors often share walls and floors, so a leak in one unit can reach a neighbor within hours.

From Extraction to Rebuild

A typical job starts with finding the source, then extraction and moisture mapping. Wet drywall, insulation and pad come out when they cannot dry, and air movers and dehumidifiers run until readings reach the dry standard. Then water damage cleanup handles debris and odor, and water damage repair rebuilds.

Condo owners should tell the association and their own insurer early, since responsibility for walls and fixtures differs by building. If damp drywall was closed up too soon, ask about mold remediation.

My neighbor's unit leaked into mine. Who do I call?

Call the restoration line first to stop the damage, then tell your association and your insurer. Responsibility depends on the source and your building's rules.

How old is too old for original plumbing?

Many 1970s and 1980s homes still have original lines and valves. Age alone does not mean failure, but a plumber can inspect and tell you what to replace.

How long will drying take?

Usually several days, depending on materials and how far water traveled. Crews take moisture readings until the structure is dry.
