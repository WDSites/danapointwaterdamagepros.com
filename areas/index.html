Dispatch is based in Dana Point, at 34085 Pacific Coast Highway, and the 24/7 line covers the city and nearby towns in Orange County. Same-day response is offered when crews are available. Each town below has its own page with local details, ZIP codes and a map.

Dana Point Neighborhoods

Home base is the city of Dana Point itself, including Lantern Village, Capistrano Beach, Monarch Beach, Niguel Shores and the Dana Point Headlands. The city's ZIP codes include 92629 and 92624. Calls here include plumbing leaks in older homes, roof leaks during the winter rain season and coastal flooding from high tides.

Laguna Niguel

Laguna Niguel, ZIP 92677, is about five miles north. It is a master-planned city with homes from the 1970s and 1980s, and the page covers aging supply lines, creeks and the Crown Valley Parkway corridor.

San Juan Capistrano

San Juan Capistrano, ZIP 92675, is about five miles northeast, where San Juan Creek and Trabuco Creek shape flood risk and the historic core has older buildings.

San Clemente

San Clemente, ZIPs 92672 and 92673, is about eight miles southeast. The page covers the rain season, stucco and tile roofs and coastal moisture.

Laguna Beach

Laguna Beach, ZIP 92651, is about ten miles northwest along the coast. The page covers canyon streets, steep lots and access for equipment.

Mission Viejo

Mission Viejo, ZIPs 92691 and 92692, is about twelve miles north. It is a larger inland city of tract homes, condos and townhomes, and the page covers aging plumbing and shared-wall leaks.
